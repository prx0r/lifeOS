## The Substitution Model

Not "study Hindi" — **replace English with Hindi**.

```
BEFORE (English ecosystem):
  bedtime → English stand-up
  YouTube → English content
  social → English friends
  errands → English interactions
  housing → tourist bubble

AFTER (Hindi ecosystem):
  bedtime → Hindi comprehensible input
  YouTube → Hindi content
  social → Hindi friends
  errands → Hindi interactions
  housing → long-term Varanasi
```

**Zero additional time has to be found.** You just change what you're already consuming.
