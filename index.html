The Life90 experiment

Make the thesis live.

One participant. One deliberately specified life. Nightly practice, daily behavior, weekly AI interrogation, objective outcomes, and competing explanations tracked together.

Research contract

The target is fixed. The interpretation is not.

The strong hypothesis is allowed to be strong: consciousness may participate creatively in the trajectory of lived reality. But the record must remain harder to fool than the belief. Every apparent hit is logged beside action-mediated, attentional, social, chance and criterion-shift explanations.

Primary final criterionAt the target age, did the frozen life-state materially occur: YES or NO, scored against the original specification? Differences are described separately rather than converted into a third outcome.

Near-term criterionDoes the practice produce measurable improvements in behavior, future-self continuity, wellbeing, opportunity detection and objective movement?

Recognition checkpointLocalization may remain while the felt status of separation changes. A positive phenomenological result does not by itself prove a universal token subject or nonlocal causation.

Research layer
Three levels of claim.
Never let a behavioral effect silently become evidence for a metaphysical one.

      

        
Layer 1 · empirical
Practice changes the participant.
Future-self simulation, implementation intentions, sleep, focused work, physical training and repeated attention can change behavior and subjective state through ordinary mechanisms.

        
Layer 2 · inexternalist
Inner and outer are aspects of one process.
The aperture is not a Cartesian spectator manipulating a separate world. A change in intrinsic organization and a change in extrinsic trajectory are descriptions of an evolving concrete process under different access conditions.

        
Layer 3 · strong hypothesis
Intention participates beyond mediation.
The experiment leaves open the stronger claim that sustained intentional configuration selects or participates in external outcomes beyond identifiable behavioral, social and attentional pathways. Only prospective evidence can move this layer.

      


      

        
        
        
        
        
      


      

        

        
Anti-self-deception protocol
What would make the strong claim less credible?
No objective movementVividness rises but bridge completion and real-world metrics do not.
All “hits” are post-hocInteresting events appear only after flexible reinterpretation; prospective predictions do not outperform expectation.
Mediation explains the pathOpportunities trace cleanly to changed search, networking, applications, skill or behavior.
Function deterioratesSleep, work, relationships or judgement worsen while metaphysical conviction rises.

      


      

        
Prospective test
Freeze it before reality answers.
Use this sparingly. Specify the event, deadline, criterion, estimated base rate and whether direct behavioral mediation is allowed.

        

      


      

        
Phenomenology / events
Log first. Explain later.
Synchronicities, dreams, opportunities and unusual states become more useful when the descriptive record is separate from the explanatory story.

        

      


      

        
Method atlas
Borrow methods without collapsing ontologies.
Each source is represented as: what it claims, what practice Life90 derives from it, and what evidential status that claim has. The traditions are allowed to disagree.

Scientific reality check

          
What is solid, and what is overstated

        

      


      

        
Supported
There is no single cognitive “peak age.”
Different abilities follow different trajectories. Processing speed, working memory, vocabulary and accumulated knowledge do not all peak together.

        
Supported
Personality keeps changing after 25.
Longitudinal meta-analyses find continued average shifts toward greater emotional stability and other maturity-related traits across adulthood, with large individual differences.

        
Supported
Strength is a reserve you can build.
Muscle mass and strength commonly peak around the early 30s, then tend to decline with age. Resistance training can preserve and improve function well into later life.

        
Nuance
Stretching is useful, but not magic.
Stretching reliably improves range of motion. Evidence is much less clear that flexibility work by itself prevents injury or produces broad health benefits. Strength through usable ranges matters too.

        
Supported
Older does not simply mean unhappier.
Many studies find better emotional regulation and greater focus on meaningful relationships with age. Findings on a universal “midlife crisis” or U-shaped happiness curve are mixed across methods and populations.

        
Myth check
“The brain finishes developing at 25” is too crude.
Large datasets show many executive functions reaching adult-level performance in late adolescence, while brain structure, connectivity, learning and behavior remain plastic across adulthood.
