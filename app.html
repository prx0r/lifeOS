Animals

Selected, rejected, and why — the distillation of the vision

The Conflicts Found

  • Rajapalayam → REJECTED: Sighthound instinct kills anything that runs. Chickens would not survive.
  • Border Collie → REJECTED: Herding instinct obsessively chases chickens. 2-3x more expensive.
  • Bakharwal → SELECTED: Same fierce loyalty, but guardian instinct protects rather than kills.
  • Principle: Every animal must coexist. The farm is an ecosystem, not independent units.
